# Write the tickets, verification, and review guide

## Intended outcome
`/docs/tickets-and-verification/` teaches the work lifecycle through one small example, keeping completion, review and dependency readiness clearly separate.

## Acceptance criteria
- One worked example ticket goes Backlog → In progress → Verification → Done, with a handoff note.
- Explains that a dependency is ready when prerequisites are in Verification or Done, and that ready does not mean accepted.
- Explains the handoff note's four parts: what changed, how it was checked, what was not verified, what to check.
- Explains that only the user decides Done; an AI can record the user's explicit decision, and that record is attributed differently from a click in the app.
- Explains review as separate from completion: unreviewed, reviewed, and changed since review.
- Explains failed verification: dependent work is flagged, nothing is reset, and the user decides the rework.
- Covers plans and archiving as they exist in the release it targets.
- Does not present any of this as tamper-proof; it explains that the files are editable Markdown and that the app makes provenance and missing attribution visible.
