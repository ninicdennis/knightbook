## Intended outcome
After creating a plan, adding a phase or attaching a ticket in the Plans view, the board shows it straight away: "View on board" filters to that plan, the board's plan menu lists it, and a just-attached ticket appears under the filter.

## Acceptance criteria
- Creating a plan in the Plans view and pressing "View on board" shows the board filtered to that plan, with the plan selected in the board's Plan menu, without Refresh or a focus change.
- Attaching a ticket to a plan and pressing "View on board" shows that ticket under the filter.
- A test fails without the fix: create a plan through the Plans view, then switch to the board and assert the plan menu lists it.

## Notes
Found in the 2026-11-10 regression pass of the packaged arm64 app on the Mac mini (macOS, AeroSpace), driven through a scratch copy of this project.

Reproduced: a new "Regression plan" with one ticket, then "View on board", left the board on "All tickets" (`usePlanFilter` drops a plan id it cannot find in `project.plans`). The board's Plan menu did not list the plan until "Refresh from disk", after which it was selected. Attaching a second ticket and pressing "View on board" again still showed only the first.

Likely cause: `app/ProjectViewSwitch.tsx` passes `onProjectChanged` to `RoutinesView` (as `onRunStarted`) but gives `PlansView` nothing, so plan writes never reload the project the board reads. Plans that already existed at load filter correctly.
