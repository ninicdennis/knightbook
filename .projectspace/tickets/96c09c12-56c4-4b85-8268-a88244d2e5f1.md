## Intended outcome
At the window's minimum width (720px) every column header shows its title, add and filter buttons, count, and column menu without clipping or overlap.

## Acceptance criteria
- At 720px wide, the Backlog header's ⋮ menu is visible and clickable, and its count does not touch the column edge.
- The other column headers stay intact at that width ("In progress" may wrap but its controls stay visible).
- Checked with `pnpm preview:board` at 720px, light and dark.

## Notes
Found in the 2026-11-10 regression pass of the packaged arm64 app on the Mac mini (macOS, AeroSpace), driven through a scratch copy of this project.

A tiling window manager such as AeroSpace easily shrinks the window to the 720px minimum. At that size the Backlog header showed "+ ▽ 15" with the count against the edge and the ⋮ menu cut off; "In progress" wrapped onto two lines. Screenshot taken with `screencapture -l` during the pass.
