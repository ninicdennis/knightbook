# Editor preservation experiment

## Intended outcome
Decide from evidence whether Milkdown can keep the no-loss Markdown requirement.

## Acceptance criteria
- No-op open is byte-identical; frontmatter, tables and code survive.
- The upstream regressions cited in the design are reproduced or refuted on the pinned version.

## Verification
Checked by the AI, not accepted by the user. Milkdown silently drops inline HTML; the merged fix is not in any published release. Configured serialization is otherwise clean. Recommendation: keep Milkdown and treat inline HTML as unsupported. See the findings.
