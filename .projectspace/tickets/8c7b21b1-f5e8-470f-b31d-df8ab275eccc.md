# State that Knightbook is free and source-available across the website

## Intended outcome
The website says plainly that Knightbook is free, names its license, and links to the source, so no visitor wonders about price or terms.

## Acceptance criteria
- The homepage FAQ answers "Is it free, and what is the license?" with the decided terms, and its `faqLicense` draft marker is removed.
- The footer's utility row gains a License link (to a short license page or the `LICENSE` file on GitHub) and a Source link to the public repository, so `utilityLinks` is no longer empty.
- The hero or download page says "Free" where a visitor decides to download, without implying open source if the license is not an OSI license.
- No copy says or implies a price, purchase, trial, account or sign-up.
- The download page links installers on public GitHub Release assets, which download without signing in.
- The production build's draft check and the site tests pass.

## Notes
Created 2026-10-21. A sweep of `apps/website` on that date found no pricing, purchase or sign-up copy; the only placeholder is the FAQ answer "The price and license are not decided yet." and the empty footer utility row. Wording such as "open source" depends on the license chosen in a677c856.
