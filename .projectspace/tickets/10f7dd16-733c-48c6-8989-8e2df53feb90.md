# Leave unsigned-app bypass steps out of the guides

## Intended outcome
The website guides say the installers are unsigned and that Windows or macOS may warn, but give no steps for getting past that warning.

## Acceptance criteria
- The installation guide no longer tells Windows users to choose **More info** then **Run anyway**.
- The installation guide no longer tells macOS users to go to **Privacy & Security** and choose **Open Anyway**.
- It still says each installer is unsigned, that a warning may appear, when to stop and report it, and not to turn off Gatekeeper for the whole Mac.
- The getting-started, connecting-AI and troubleshooting guides give no such steps either.

## Notes
The user's decision (2026-10-21): guides leave out the steps for getting past unsigned-app warnings. The installation guide (ticket [6281905f](6281905f-048f-4df8-8d76-62e183ecb782.md)) still had them. The troubleshooting guide already says it gives no such steps. Website only; nothing in the app changes, so it releases nothing (D-041).

## Handoff
**What changed:**
- `apps/website/src/content/docs/installation.md`: the Windows section no longer says to choose **More info** then **Run anyway**, and the macOS section no longer says to go to **Privacy & Security** and choose **Open Anyway**. Both now say the installer is unsigned, that a warning may appear, that the guide gives no steps for getting past it and the choice is the reader's, and when to stop and report it. "Do not turn off Gatekeeper…" stays. The macOS "or offers no way to open it" report trigger went too, since with no bypass steps that is the expected outcome.
- `apps/website/src/docs/docs.test.ts`: a new test, run for the installation, getting-started, connecting-AI and troubleshooting guides, fails if the built page contains "Run anyway", "Open Anyway", "More info" or "Privacy & Security".
- The getting-started, connecting-AI and troubleshooting guides had no such steps (searched), so they are unchanged.

**How it was checked:**
- The new test fails for the installation guide with the guide change stashed, and passes with it.
- `pnpm check` passed: website 208 passed, 37 skipped; desktop 666; core 281; mcp 47; packaging 46 passed, 9 skipped.
- Criterion 3 was settled by reading the diff; the existing "never tells readers to turn off a protection" test still checks the Gatekeeper sentence and "report it".

**Not verified:**
- The `verifier` agent is not available in this cloud session, so it did not run; the criteria were checked by hand as above.
- Packaged-app and site-accessibility tests were skipped (nothing packaged, no browser run), as on any website-only change. The page was not looked at in a browser.

**For you to check:**
- Read the Windows and macOS "Installing" sections of `/docs/installation/` and confirm the wording is what you want.
