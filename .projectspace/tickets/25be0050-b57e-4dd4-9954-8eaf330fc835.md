# Validate the in-app half of the specification scenario

## Intended outcome
The parts of the nineteen-step scenario that need the real window and a person are checked, so the scenario is validated end to end rather than only through MCP.

## Acceptance criteria
- Step 4: accepting a ticket directly in the app records a completion, and it is distinguishable from one an AI recorded on instruction.
- Step 6: an external edit to a document appears in the running UI, on focus and via Refresh.
- Step 7: the documentation generated while onboarding an existing app reads correctly in the app.
- Step 11: after MCP changes made with the app closed, reopening shows them and distinguishes the user's decision from AI-reported checks.
- Step 18: with a ticket marked reviewed in the app, a meaningful content change flags it for renewed review and keeps the prior review; a formatting-only change does not reset it.
- Each recorded pass or fail with evidence, appended to docs/validation-scenario-results.md.

## Notes
Found running the specification validation scenario (ticket 2e4a254f). The MCP-observable steps are recorded in docs/validation-scenario-results.md; these are the ones deliberately left out.

Step 18 cannot be set up from MCP at all: recording a review is app-only by design (`review_status` reads, nothing writes), so the reviewed -> changed_since_review transition needs a person in the app before it can be observed. That is the design working as intended, not a defect, but it does mean step 18 is currently unvalidated.
