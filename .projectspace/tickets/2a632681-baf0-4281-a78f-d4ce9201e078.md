## Intended outcome
Every dialog's close (X) button is announced by name, such as "Close", by screen readers and is reachable by name in tests.

## Acceptance criteria
- The header close button of every Mantine `Modal` in the renderer has an accessible name.
- A test opens at least New ticket and Settings and finds the close button by role and name.

## Notes
Found in the 2026-11-10 regression pass of the packaged arm64 app on the Mac mini (macOS, AeroSpace), driven through a scratch copy of this project.

The accessibility tree shows an unnamed `button` in the header of New ticket, Accept this as done?, Settings, New routine, New plan and Add a phase. The ticket detail dialog has its own labelled "Close". No `Modal` in `apps/desktop/src/renderer` sets `closeButtonProps`; one shared default (theme or wrapper) would cover all 15.
