# Show unmet prerequisites on cards, and "Unlocked" when there are none

## Intended outcome
A card's dependency badge says how many prerequisites are still unmet, and when every prerequisite is met it shows a green "Unlocked" badge instead, so a ticket whose prerequisites are finished never reads as waiting on them.

## Acceptance criteria
- "Waiting on N" counts only prerequisites that do not satisfy the dependency (not in Verification or Done, missing, unreadable, archived while unfinished, or failed verification per D-017), not every prerequisite listed.
- A ticket with prerequisites, all of them satisfied, shows a green "Unlocked" badge; its tooltip says they are in Verification or Done and that this does not mean they were accepted (D-014).
- A ticket with no prerequisites shows neither badge; "Waiting on 0" never appears.
- A blocker, a failed-verification flag or a dependency cycle is still shown by its own badge, and does not turn into or inflate "Waiting on N".
- The count and the Unlocked state update right after a prerequisite moves, without a refresh.

## Notes
Found by the user on 2026-09-29, after both prerequisites of one of their tickets were done but its card still said it was waiting.

What the code does today (`apps/desktop/src/renderer/board/TicketCardBadges.tsx`): the badge shows when `status === 'backlog' && !dependencyReady` and its label is `dependsOn.length`, the total. `dependencyReady` is false for any readiness reason, including a blocker (`evaluateReadiness` in `packages/core/src/tickets/dependencies.ts` adds a `blocked` reason). So:
- "Build and publish a GitHub Release from the release branch" (356c7683) shows "Waiting on 2" beside "Blocked": its prerequisites are satisfied and only its "Deferred" blocker keeps it from being ready.
- "Formatted in-app Markdown editor" (b217a95f) shows "Waiting on 0" beside "Blocked": it has no prerequisites at all.

The card's `TicketView` has no per-prerequisite states, so the unmet count likely needs a field computed by the host from the readiness reasons (one source of truth, core), rather than re-derived in the renderer.

Open for the user to decide when starting: whether "Unlocked" shows only in Backlog (where readiness matters for "next up") or in every column, and its exact wording.

## Handoff
**What changed:** The user chose Backlog only and the word "Unlocked" (recorded as D-033 in the spec; technical design section on dependencies updated). Core has `summarizePrerequisites` (`tickets/dependencies.ts`): the unmet prerequisites (not ready, archived unfinished, missing, unreadable, or directly failed verification, each counted once) and whether all are met (has prerequisites, and no reason other than a blocker). The host adds `unmetPrerequisites`, `prerequisitesMet` and `dependencyCycle` to `TicketView`. The card shows "Waiting on <unmet>", a green "Unlocked" with a not-acceptance tooltip, and a new orange "Dependency cycle" badge, all in Backlog only. "Unlocked" is not shown for a ticket affected by a failure or in a cycle. Stub board data gained an Unlocked + Blocked example.
**How it was checked:** `pnpm check` passes. New tests: `packages/core/src/tickets/prerequisite-summary.test.ts` (10, including unreadable, archived, missing, failed, reopened-and-failed counted once, blocker, no prerequisites, upstream failure, cycle, self-dependency, the ticket's own failure), `main/tickets/ticket-view.test.ts` (2, real files: count drops as each prerequisite moves to Verification), `renderer/board/Prerequisites.test.tsx` (8: counts unmet not total, Unlocked beside Blocked and green, tooltip wording, no badge without prerequisites, failure badge beside a count of 1, cycle badge, not shown in In progress, Waiting turns into Unlocked after a move with no re-read). Board preview looked at 1600px dark and 800px light: badges wrap cleanly beside the type, review and Blocked badges. Verifier: 2 covered, 3 weakly covered; the gaps it named (unreadable, self-dependency, own failure, card-level failure, colour) were then covered by the added tests above.
**Not verified:** Seen in the board preview only, against stub data, not in the app with a real project. In the move test the host's reply is a fixture; the recount itself is covered by the ticket-view test on real files. MCP's `ticket_list` is unchanged and does not report the count.
**For you to check:** Rebuild and open the app on this project: 356c7683 should read "Unlocked" + "Blocked", b217a95f only "Blocked". Move a prerequisite of a waiting ticket to Verification and watch its count drop.
