# Document viewer

## Intended outcome
Read included documents in the app, treating their content as untrusted (design section 1).

## Acceptance criteria
- Scripts, handlers, iframes and inline styles are stripped.
- Only http and https links are clickable; every other scheme renders as inert text.
- Images are not fetched. Frontmatter is shown apart from the body.

## Verification
Checked by the AI, not accepted by the user. 13 renderer tests cover link classification and sanitizing; the page was checked in Chromium.
Not verified: the view inside the packaged window, and opening an external link through the real shell.
