# Packaged-app smoke test

## Intended outcome
Catch regressions in the real Electron window, which no current test touches.

## Acceptance criteria
- A Playwright Electron test launches the packaged app, opens a fixture project, moves a card, and asserts the result on disk.
- A keyboard-only pass over the card menu and the completion dialog.

## Notes
Design section 2 calls Playwright's Electron support experimental. A screenshot of the packaged window could not be captured in the environment used so far.
