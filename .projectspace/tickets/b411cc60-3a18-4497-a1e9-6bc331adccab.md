# Show the app version inside the app

## Intended outcome
A person reporting a problem can read which Knightbook version they are running from the app itself.

## Acceptance criteria
- The running version (from the package, as `app.getVersion()` reports it) is shown in one findable place, such as the settings menu or an About entry.
- The bug-report template and the troubleshooting guide say where to find it.
