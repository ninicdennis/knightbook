# Set and clear a ticket's blocker in the app

## Intended outcome
The user can record why a ticket cannot move, or clear that reason, from the ticket detail view.

## Acceptance criteria
- The detail view has a control to add, edit and remove the blocker text.
- Clearing the blocker stores no blocker (null), not an empty string.
- The card's blocker badge appears and disappears to match, without a manual refresh.
- The write carries the ticket's revision and handles a conflict the same way other ticket edits do.

## Notes
Today the blocker is displayed on cards and in the detail view but can only be written through MCP or by hand.
