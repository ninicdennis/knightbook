# Reopening a Done ticket

## Intended outcome
Moving a Done ticket back leaves no misleading acceptance record.

## Acceptance criteria
- Transition rules are written down; design section 5 says they need to be explicit.
- Decide whether completion provenance is cleared or archived when a ticket leaves Done.
- No card shows Accepted by you, or Recorded by AI, on a ticket that is not Done.
- A test covers it.

## Notes
Reading updateTicket suggests a ticket moved out of Done keeps its completion record, and the card shows that badge whatever the status. Reproduce before fixing.
