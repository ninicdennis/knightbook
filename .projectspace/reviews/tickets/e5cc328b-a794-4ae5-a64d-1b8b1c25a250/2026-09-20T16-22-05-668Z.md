# Clear reading hierarchy

## Intended outcome
Ticket descriptions and project documents are comfortable to scan and read, with a stronger title hierarchy and quieter supporting information.

## Acceptance criteria
- Keep the existing system font and establish a consistent scale for dialog titles, authored headings, prose, links, lists, and metadata using the design-direction document as the starting point.
- Ticket titles remain visually stronger than body headings. Preserve every authored heading and paragraph even when the body repeats its title; do not rewrite Markdown to achieve the visual result.
- Retain roughly 65–78ch prose measure and comfortable line/paragraph/list spacing. Check a long acceptance-criteria list, nested lists, quotations, and multiple heading levels.
- Use shared blue-gray surfaces for code and tables, readable link styling, and local horizontal scrolling for genuinely wide tables/code. Long URLs and unbroken text cannot force the whole dialog or document pane wider.
- Reach the design document's contrast targets in both schemes; quiet metadata and related-document links remain legible. Focus and links remain distinguishable without color alone.
- Preserve sanitization, blocked link/image indicators, frontmatter visibility, and all authored content. Apply equivalent reading styles to ticket and document contexts without leaking CSS into board cards.
- Record the content styling contract for the future formatted editor; implement no editor engine, serialization changes, or rich-text saving.
